## Son üretim istemi

Use case: logo-brand. Create a refined professional HAYA university aviation and AI research community logo using the attached original as an identity reference. One clean flat logo on a perfectly uniform solid very pale lavender background #eef0fd, no transparency. The emblem is a balanced top-down quadcopter: four diagonal arms meeting at the center, thin circular rotor outlines at the four outer tips. Yellow upper-left, red upper-right, green lower-left, blue lower-right. Improve the geometry over the reference: slightly slimmer equal-width arms, consistent rounded tips, evenly weighted rotor rings, precise symmetry, calm balanced proportions, plenty of clear negative space. No tiny rainbow dash. Under the symbol, exact uppercase text HAYA in a confident custom geometric italic navy #202647 wordmark, generous precise letter spacing, wide and centered. Four letters H A Y A only, no accents, no extra text. Sharp flat solid colors and clean vector-style edges, no gradients, textures, speckle, noise, shadows, glow, 3D, photograph or mockup. Symbol and wordmark vertically stacked, one single logo, square canvas, modest balanced padding. This will be used on a light website header and must look like professionally crafted vector identity artwork.
